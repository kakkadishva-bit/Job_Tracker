# Interview Knowledge Base - Behavioral & Role Guidance

## Behavioral Interview Framework

**STAR Method**: Situation, Task, Action, Result. Always structure behavioral answers with this framework. The interviewer wants to see your problem-solving process, not just the outcome.

**Common Behavioral Questions & Strategies**:

- "Tell me about yourself": Focus on professional narrative connecting past experience to this role. Keep it concise (2 minutes).
- "Why do you want to work here?": Reference specific company products/technologies. Connect your skills to their needs.
- "What's your greatest weakness?": Choose a real but non-critical weakness. Show active improvement steps.
- "Describe a conflict with a teammate": Emphasize resolution, communication, and learning. Avoid blame.

## Project Discussion

When discussing projects, always cover:
1. **Context**: Problem you were solving
2. **Your role**: Specific contributions (not team efforts)
3. **Technical decisions**: Why you chose specific technologies
4. **Challenges**: Obstacles encountered and how you overcame them
5. **Outcome**: Measurable results or learnings
6. **Trade-offs**: What you'd do differently

## Role-Specific Guidance

**AI/ML Engineer**: Expect deep technical questions on algorithms, model selection, evaluation metrics, MLOps. Be ready to discuss specific projects, trade-offs between models, and your role in deployment.

**Backend Engineer**: Focus on system design, API design, database optimization, concurrency, scalability. Be prepared to write code for common problems (rate limiting, pagination, caching).

**Full Stack Developer**: Expect questions spanning frontend (React patterns, state management) and backend (REST APIs, databases). Discuss your approach to full-stack architecture.

## Cultural Fit Assessment

Interviewers evaluate:
- **Growth mindset**: How you learn and adapt
- **Collaboration**: Working with cross-functional teams
- **Autonomy**: Taking ownership of problems
- **Communication**: Explaining technical concepts clearly
- **Problem-solving**: Decomposing complex problems

Showcase these qualities with concrete examples from your experience.
